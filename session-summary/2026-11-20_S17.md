## 3. Technical Learnings
- **The 2000 caps aren't in the report.** The 2000 two-year averaging plus the 5% residential and 10% NR growth caps came under a separate "Equalized Assessment Variance Regulation" for school requisitions (*Advisory Aspects* No. 6, 1999-12-22, p4). The report prints the uncapped figure.
- **The pre-2000 cap rule is unsourced.** Calgary, Airdrie, Edmonton and others appealed their 1997–1999 equalized assessments to the MGB as unfair.
- **FIR EA 1998–1999 equals the PDFs**, so it is capped too and offers no uncapped alternative. FIR 2000 is 4–6% below the PDF for both cores and within 1% for the rings. That is unexplained, and the series doesn't use FIR for 2000.
- **Devon (1999) and Sturgeon (2001) are already corrected from FIR** (`data/corrections.csv`, DECISIONS 2026-10-02). The 2026-09-28 findings describe them as uncorrected, so read DECISIONS first.
- **Gotcha: overflow in a mutation.** Multiplying int64 columns of `core_ring_share.csv` in pandas overflows. The first mutation went red on a garbage value. Use floats.
- **Gotcha: fetching on this box.** `municipalaffairs.alberta.ca` / `.gov.ab.ca` reset WebFetch connections. A plain `curl` to open.alberta.ca worked where WebFetch got 520. `.venv` has no `certifi`.
