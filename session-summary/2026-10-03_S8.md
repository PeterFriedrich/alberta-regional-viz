## 3. Technical Learnings
- **What the bigger picture now looks like:** each Phase 2/2b per-capita series
  divides FIR dollars (each year's own boundaries) by StatCan population (all
  years back-cast to 2021 boundaries). They use different boundaries, which is
  audit Q1.
- **FIR Schedule E function codes:** 02250 police, 02350 transit, 02440 FCSS,
  02520 housing, constant 1994–2025. Expense codes in C: 01210, 01310, 01400,
  01480. 2001 maps by name (`FUNCTION_NAMES`).
- **Pre-2009 Schedule D Operating has an amortization line (02110)**, and
  every member reports $0 in it for 2003–08. Cash Operating carries no
  amortization.
- **The annual reports differ in what each function line includes.**
  Edmonton's include amortization; Calgary's put amortization and interest on
  separate lines. Edmonton's transit and community lines are a different scope
  from the FIR's.
- **Fetching:** edmonton.ca serves its annual reports from 2010 only. From this
  box it needs `--cacert /home/opc/edmonton-tax-viz/.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/certifi/cacert.pem`
  (this repo's venv has no certifi). Wayback `id_` URLs work (`https://web.archive.org/web/2011id_/<url>`),
  and the CDX API finds old PDFs.
- **StatCan publishes no CSD population estimates before 2001**
  (`getAllCubesListLite`: only 17-10-0142 and 17-10-0155, both from 2001).
- **The BRIEF is generated from TODO.md and DATA_ISSUES.md** (among others).
  Any edit to either needs `make_brief.py --write`, or `test_brief` fails in CI.
  That broke #20's CI once this session.
- Open item added: Calgary FCSS swings as printed ($233M in 2022, $185M,
  $136M, $245M in 2025).
