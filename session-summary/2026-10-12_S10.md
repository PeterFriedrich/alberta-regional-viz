## 3. Technical Learnings
- **FIR function splits are filer-coded and unaudited; totals are solid.** A single member-year spike on a "fuzzy" line (FCSS, housing, planning, economic development, land & housing rentals) is not evidence of spending by itself.
  - Test 1: does a neighbouring line offset it, on both expense *and* revenue?
  - Test 2: does the audited report show the same move?
  - Police and transit don't show this.
- **Full Schedule C for any city:** use the research folder's `schedC_all.py`. It patches `parse_fir.wanted` to read every line. Run it from the repo root.
  - FIR codes in the sheets are zero-padded (`"0046"`), so match with `code.lstrip("0")`.
  - Accrual sheet layout: revenue is 00960–01140, expense 01180–01580. 01040 is housing *revenue* and 01480 is housing *expense*.
- **www.edmonton.ca fails TLS from this box** ("self signed certificate in certificate chain"; the cert is Entrust, so the OL8 CA bundle is the likely cause). Fetch Edmonton PDFs through Wayback: `https://web.archive.org/web/<yr>id_/<url>`. calgary.ca works directly.
- **Calgary annual report URLs:**
  - 2018–2022: `.../plans-budgets-and-financial-reports/annual-reports/annual-report-<yr>.pdf`
  - 2023, 2024: `.../cfod/finance/documents/Finance-<yr>-Annual-Report.pdf`
  - 2025: `.../2025-Annual-Financial-Report.pdf`
- **CI trap, hit once this session:** `docs/BRIEF.md` is generated from `CLAUDE.md`, `requirements.txt`, DECISIONS and DATA_ISSUES. After editing any of them, run `.venv/bin/python scripts/make_brief.py --write`. `tests/test_brief.py` fails otherwise.
- **Notebook rebuild:** `.venv/bin/jupytext --sync notebooks/01_first_look.py`, then `.venv/bin/jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute --inplace notebooks/01_first_look.ipynb`.
  - Edit the `.py`.

  - In a 4×2 `sharey="row"` grid, set `ylim(bottom=0)` only after both panels are drawn. Otherwise the limit freezes on the first panel and clips the second (this happened to Calgary FCSS).
