## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Absorbed villages, with FIR codes and last Schedule D year:** Blackie 0032
  (1997), Entwistle 0104 (2000), New Sarepta 0234 (2009), Wabamun 0364 (2020).
  - Their EA rows run later than their D rows (FIR year Y carries report year Y).
  - EA keeps **all-zero placeholder rows** for Blackie and Entwistle to 2004, so
    `check_complete` spans an absorbed code to its last **non-zero** year per
    schedule.
- **Wabamun** carried up to $104M NR (the power plant). It dissolved into
  Parkland on 2021-01-01 (StatCan 92F0009X 2021).
- **17-10-0155:** join on `DGUID[-7:]` (`2021A0005` + CSD). The estimates run
  0–6% above census counts. Never mix the two in one series.
- **FIR Schedule C** function codes are constant 1994–2025: revenue
  00770/00870/00960/01040, expense 01210/01310/01400/01480.
  - Pre-2009 there are separate Operating, Capital and Total files (2002–03 are
    split REVENUE/EXPENDITURE `.xls`; 2001 has `C_col*` files with no code row).
  - From 2009 there is one `C(1)-Revenue` sheet (REVENUE/EXPENSE - TOTAL), and
    Schedule E `E(3)-Amortization` has amortization by function.
  - Schedule E sales and user charges by function exist 1994–2025 (codes
    02250/02350/02440/02520).
- **Rural police:** Parkland, Rocky View and Sturgeon report $0 police in every
  year 2017–2023, and Foothills does until 2021. Police Funding Model payments
  are not in any obvious protective-services line.
- **Spruce Grove 2015** provincial conditional transfers are −$1,760,210. Kept
  as printed and named in `basis_note`.

- **Falsify only after committing.** A `cp` backup restore failed once this
