## 3. Technical Learnings
- **eSCRIBE endpoints that work without a browser:**
  - `POST /MeetingsCalendarView.aspx/GetCalendarMeetings` with body `{"calendarStartDate":"YYYY-MM-DD","calendarEndDate":"YYYY-MM-DD"}` and `Content-Type: application/json; charset=utf-8`; the list is under key `d`.
  - `GET /Meeting.aspx?Id=<guid>&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English` (or `Agenda=PostMinutes`).
  - The agenda HTML names every attachment and its `DocumentId`, so a report number or document ID can be found without the PDF.
